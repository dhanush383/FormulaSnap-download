FormulaSnap - snip handwritten or printed notes into Word
=========================================================

What it does
------------
Snip any part of the screen (notes, formulas, diagrams). FormulaSnap reads it and
puts it into Microsoft Word as:
  - clean typed text (your document's font, headings in bold)
  - real Word equations (fractions, powers, roots... editable)
  - diagrams redrawn as editable Word drawings
  - circuits drawn with exact textbook symbols (resistors, capacitors, inductors,
    diodes, BJTs, MOSFETs, op-amps, logic gates, transformers, sources, meters,
    ground/Vcc). Each part is one object: click it to move, copy or delete it.

Diagram settings (bottom of the window)
---------------------------------------
  Diagrams: Editable drawing      - redrawn, you can edit every line and label
            Exact picture (cleaned) - the original diagram, shadows removed,
                                      white paper, sharp ink (looks 100% the same)
            Both                  - the clean picture AND the editable drawing
  Double-check diagrams - after reading, Claude compares the redrawing with the
            original and fixes anything wrong (wrong part, wire, label, value).
            More accurate; takes a little longer and costs a little more.

AI model (bottom of the window)
-------------------------------
  Economy - Haiku (cheapest)   about Rs 1-2 per circuit or page. Good for most work.
  Balanced - Sonnet            middle price.
  Best - Opus (most accurate)  costs much more; use for big, messy circuits
                               or when Economy gets something wrong.
  "Spent: $..." on the right shows what this session has cost so far.

This PC needs
-------------
  - Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)   (Windows 7 / 8 are not supported)
  - Microsoft Word 2010, 2013, 2016, 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365 (desktop app,
    32-bit or 64-bit). Word 2007 also works for most things.
  - Your document must be a .docx file. Old .doc files (Word 97-2003 format)
    cannot hold equations - in Word use File > Save As > "Word Document (*.docx)".
  - Internet connection
  - A Claude API key (from console.anthropic.com). Each snip costs a small amount
    from that account. The key is saved only on this PC.

Nothing else needs to be installed. Python is NOT needed.

First time
----------
1. Copy FormulaSnap.exe to a folder that will stay (for example Documents).
   Don't run it straight from Downloads or from a pen drive.
2. Double-click FormulaSnap.exe.
   If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (This appears because the app is new and not signed by a company.)
3. Paste your Claude API key when asked.
4. Tick "Start with Windows" at the bottom of the window if you want it to open
   every time the PC starts.

Every day
---------
1. Open your Word document normally and click where the result should go.
2. Press  Win + Shift + S  and select the notes / formula / diagram.
3. FormulaSnap pops up and reads it (a full page with diagrams takes 1-3 minutes).
4. Check the result box (you can correct mistakes there), then press
   "Insert into Word"   - or -   "Copy for Word" and press Ctrl+V in Word.

Convert a whole file (PDF or photos of pages)
---------------------------------------------
1. Press "Convert file" at the top of the window.
2. Choose a PDF, or one or more photos/scans of your pages (several photos are
   taken in name order: page1.jpg, page2.jpg ...).
3. A chat window opens. If you want something special, type it first
   (e.g. "skip pages 1-2", "Times New Roman 12", "double spacing"), then press
   "Start converting".
4. It reads every page, WORD FOR WORD (never a summary), and makes a Word file
   that LOOKS LIKE YOUR ORIGINAL:
     - every word, heading, bullet and table, in the same order
     - bold / underlined / centred text kept, two-column parts kept
     - the same font, one Word page per original page
     - equations as real, editable Word equations
     - diagrams redrawn as editable Word drawings
   For PDFs with text inside, it checks the word count against the PDF and
   reads a page again if anything is missing ("PDF text check: 208/208 words").
5. The new file is saved next to your original as  "<name> - typed.docx"
   and opens in Word by itself. Your original file is never changed.
   Time: about 15-60 seconds per page. Press "Stop" to cancel.
   A page that can't be read is added as a picture, so nothing is lost.

Question paper (Exam paper button)
----------------------------------
1. Press "Exam paper" at the top of the window.
2. Choose the pattern:
     ANITS - one question per unit, with OR   (marks as 4M, no table lines)
     GVP / AP autonomous R23 - Part A + units, CO & BL columns (table)
   Fill in college, examination, regulation, subject, branch, code, time, marks.
3. Type the questions, one line each, like:
     UNIT-I
     1. a) Explain the working of a bridge rectifier. [6M] [CO1] [L2]
        b) Find the ripple factor $\gamma = \frac{1}{4\sqrt{3} f C R_L}$  [6M] [CO1] [L3]
     OR
     2. Derive the diode current equation. [12M] [CO1] [L3]
   (maths between $...$; CO and level are optional for ANITS)
   Or press "Snip questions" (select questions/circuits on screen) or
   "Import old paper / photo" - Claude types them in this format for you,
   circuits included. Before anything is sent, FormulaSnap reminds you that
   the picture goes to the Claude AI (check your college allows it for
   unpublished papers; old/released papers are fine).
   A REVIEW window then shows the original on the left and what was read on
   the right. Anything the AI was not sure about is marked [?] in yellow.
   Fix it, then press "Approve - add to my paper" (nothing is added before).
   Tick "This is an old paper" to keep its questions in your PAST-PAPERS
   LIBRARY: "Past papers" searches them (and adds one to your paper, circuit
   included), and "Check my paper" warns when a question was asked before.
4. Press "Check my paper". It checks, without AI:
     - marks add up to Max. Marks
     - OR choices carry equal marks, every unit has two choices
     - question numbers have no gaps or repeats, parts are a, b, c...
     - every part has marks (and CO + level for the R23 pattern)
     - repeated / near-identical questions
     - CO and Bloom-level summary
5. Press "Make Word paper". The paper (header, units, OR, marks column,
   real equations, editable circuits) opens in Word and is saved in
   Documents\FormulaSnap papers, with a PDF copy to check before printing.
Your draft is kept automatically. Nothing is sent to the internet unless you
use Snip or Import (unpublished questions you type stay on this PC).

Chat (tell it what to change)
-----------------------------
The chat window shows, step by step, what FormulaSnap is doing. After the file
is made, type what you want and press Send (or Enter), for example:
   - "page 3 is missing the last paragraph, add it"
   - "fix the formula under Bernoulli's theorem"
   - "make the headings bigger" / "Times New Roman 12, justified"
   - "add a short explanation after each formula"
   - "make it designed" (title page, contents, coloured headings, page numbers)
   - "what is on page 4?"
Each change is saved as a new version (… - typed (2).docx); older versions are
kept. The main window's "Activity" box shows the same progress.

Other buttons
-------------
  Insert snip as picture  - puts the screenshot itself into Word.
  Copy LaTeX              - copies the LaTeX text.
  API key                 - change the key.

If something goes wrong
-----------------------
  - "Word is running as administrator": close Word and open it normally (not with
    right-click > Run as administrator). Until then, the app copies the result
    for you - just press Ctrl+V in Word.
  - "old Word 97-2003 (.doc) format": save the document as .docx first
    (File > Save As > Word Document), then press Insert again.
  - Word 2010: if the title bar says "[Compatibility Mode]", use
    File > Info > Convert, so equations stay editable.
  - "API key is wrong": click "API key" at the bottom and paste a valid key.
  - Error details are saved in:  %APPDATA%\FormulaSnap\log.txt
